## Team Strategy

Strategically building a team is very important for a startup.

Address these questions to avoid the pitfalls of team building and maximize the likelihood of success for you, your team, and your startup:

### Who is involved in creating and selling your first products?

Typical initial team structure in today's agile, high-performance startups is:
- 1-3 co-founders with complementary skills and a common interest in the startup's mission
- Membership in an incubator environment, such as a university-based entrepreneurship program or a local co-working space
- Several active advisers that bring experience, wisdom, and relationships to the team
- Multiple startup peers that you can learn from and share with
- Minimal investment of \$0 to \$100,000

This team needs to be able to understand the [[Value Proposition|value proposition]] elements and have the ability to build and test the MVP to achieve [[Product-Market Fit|product-market fit]].

Primary goals for the team in the first 3-6 months:
- Demonstrate the product or service concept
- Reduce product risk
- Test functional use
- Develop metrics
- Collect data to support future fundraising efforts

### Who is your Minimum Viable Team?

- Hacker: An engineer and developer who can build the functional prototype and alpha
- Hipster: A person with design and user experience that can improve UX and conversion
- Hustler: Business/sales/marketing person who can find a way to sell the product or service, scale customer acquisition, and distribute the product or service

Most startup teams have limited professional experience, money, professional connections, and no experience in launching/growing a successful startup.

### What business are you in?

It's important to establish what business you are in, specifically what activities will be your focus and what activities will you achieve through partners.

These activities vary based on the business model of the company.

### Questions to Consider

- Based on your value propositions, what kinds of activities are key to your startup?
- What kinds of activities are key to your distribution channels?
- What kinds of activities are important to develop and maintain customer relationships?
- What kinds of activities are fundamental to your revenue streams?

### Typical Startup Activities for the Team to Lead

##### Research & Development (R&D)

Typical functions:
- New product research
- New product development
- Existing product updates
- Quality checks
- Innovation

##### Production

Activities:
- Selection of product and design
- Selection of production process
- Selecting the right production capacity
- Production planning
- Production control
- Quality and cost control
- Inventory control
- Maintenance and replacement of machines

##### Marketing

Activities:
- Strategy
- Market research
- Product development
- Communications
- Sales support
- Events

##### Sales and Customer Services

Responsibilities:
- Handling problems
- Assisting in sales
- Clerical tasks

## Founders

Finding one or more co-founders is typically the best avenue to building the initial team.

### Where do you find co-founders?

Friends and family aren't the best idea since they typically lack the experience and commitment necessary and also bring pre-existing personal relationships that may complicate the professional relationship.

Seeking fellow students is a good method for student entrepreneurs, particularly those from prior courses, internships, and jobs.

Networking to find co-founders is also a good option.

### What are the prerequisites for co-founders?

A good initial team of co-founders have complementary skillsets (different skills) and shared vision and values.

### What are they key traits of co-founders?

- Eagerness to learn
- Self-sufficiency
- Empathy
- Emotional intelligence
- Ability to work through disagreement

### Why is trust fundamental?

Finding someone who is honest is important as it is possible for co-founders to embezzle funds, cheat/lie, or engage in illegal activities.

This is why having a shared vision and values is important.

### Will you find the perfect co-founder?

It's unlikely that you will find the perfect-cofounder. It's possible to join with another co-founder and reorganize the company or start another company if a co-founder is a flop.

### Are there gaps in the skillsets of your founding team?

If the founding team lacks critical skillsets for the creation and launch of the product or service, we could add more founders or hire freelancers as well (from sites like Upwork).

### What systems and structures will you develop?

1. Distribute responsibility - Push power downward, across the organization, empowering people at all levels to make decisions
2. Be honest and open about information
3. Create multiple paths for raising and testing ideas - set up ways for people to bring their innovative thinking to the surface

### What people, policies, and practices will you integrate?

- Make it safe to fail - Enshrine acceptance of failure and willingness to admit failure early in the practices and processes of the startup
- Provide access to other strategists - give potential strategic leaders the opportunity to meet and work with their peers inside and outside of the company
- Hire for transformation

### How will you develop your skills to lead?

- Bring your whole self to work
- Find time to reflect
- Recognize leadership development as an ongoing practice

### Operating Agreement

Create an operating agreement for your founders, which defines the roles and responsibilities that each of the founders will play in the creation and growth of your company.

Seek legal counsel to develop this.
