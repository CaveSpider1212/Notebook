### Vocal Delivery

**Rate**: tends to speed up naturally due to adrenaline, so speak slower than you think

Also, have audible pauses every 20-30 seconds. You want your audience to stop and think for a moment, and also helps to slow down the rate.

**Vocal fillers**: "um", "uh", "like", etc. (keep these as minimal as possible)

**Volume**: tends to speak quieter, so speak louder than you think

**Pitch (tone)**: speak in a conversational pitch/tone, as if you're talking to your friends in the room next to you

**Inflection**: which word the vocal emphasis goes on makes a difference

### Physical Delivery

**Eye contact**: keep eye contact with your audience roughly 70% of the time

**Facial expressions**: smile first

**Movement and gestures**:  movement and gestures (or lack of) can make a difference

Illustrators are gestures that substitute for or complement a verbal message.
